# Decision log

Copy to `docs/context/decision-log.md` during a kickoff when there is at least
one decision. One entry per decision, newest first. A feature brief still owns
its current decisions; this log keeps the reasons that outlive one feature.

## <Decision title>

- **Status:** decided | proposed | not established
- **Decision:** what was chosen, or the preference the user stated.
- **Why:** the user's reason, quoted where possible.
- **Rejected:** the options considered and why they lost.
- **Source:** [kickoff notes](kickoff-notes.md) or the brief that owns it.
