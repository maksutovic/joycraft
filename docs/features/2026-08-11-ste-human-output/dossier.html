D3 · VIOLATION THRESHOLD

What does the self-check oblige the agent to fix?

The regex is reliable on some classes (contractions, semicolons, banned modals) and approximate on others (sentence bounds, synonym rotation). A single rule that treats them alike is either too lax or falsely precise.

Two-tier: fix-to-zero + advisory
