Boundaries

What the implementation must and must not do

Must
  • Tag every telemetry read mandated or voluntary; only voluntary reads feed retire/keep evidence
  • Derive external paths at runtime (frontmatter.ts idiom) — no literal absolute paths in shipped text
  • Keep telemetry.json machine-local and gitignored; counts only, never transcript content
  • Write the 7-day staleness rule once, in knowledge-lifecycle.md; skills cite it
  • Write Product Identity elicit-first — only when the interview collected real content
  • Pay for additions to over-budget skills with same-commit trims or citations
  • Spare joycraft-owner.txt in any auto-memory cleanup step
Must not
  • Auto-delete anything — staleness flags and RETIRE evidence are advisory
  • Grow the evidence vocabulary past seven or add synonyms
  • Scaffold TODO stubs for the Product Identity section at init
  • Ship a hand-maintained architecture tree — the map regenerates from the filesystem
  • Touch the user's global settings — auto-memory disable is per-project, offered not imposed
  • Claim Codex telemetry parity — it is labeled best-effort, degraded
  • Build embeddings, graphs, or retrieval machinery (brief non-goal, panel-affirmed)
