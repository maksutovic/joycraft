Context

The shape the evidence points at

Seven of eight reports converge on the same four moves. The panel exists to attack them, not to adopt them. The full argument and every citation live in the evidence pack.

Hub plus a compact router

One entry skill and a small visible core. Superpowers deleted its slash commands; BMAD cut to eight skills behind a hub; Vercel measured an always-loaded 8KB index at 100% invocation where skills alone hit 53%.

The CLI as state oracle

Skills ask the CLI what exists and what is next. OpenSpec did this in January. Today ten Joycraft skills hand off through the human with "clear the session, paste this briefing."

Gates, not advice

Helix: "An attempt is allowed to be wrong. It is not allowed to ship until it isn't." Every study where generated artifacts helped had an acceptance gate. Without one: null results, check-gaming, peak-then-decline.

Less process, same verification

Explicit planning helped a 30B model and slightly hurt the strongest one. Anthropic: capability-uplift skills die as models improve; encoded team-workflow skills persist; fresh-context verifiers beat self-critique.
